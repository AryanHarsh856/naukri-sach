💡 How to Spot a Fake Job

Learn the warning signs before it is too late.

💸

They Ask for Money

Real companies NEVER ask you to pay registration fees, deposits, or any charges to join.

📧

Gmail HR Email

Legitimate companies use their own domain email like hr@company.com — not Gmail or Yahoo.

🚀

Too Good to Be True

Offers promising huge salary with no experience and no interview are almost always fake.

⏰

Fake Urgency

Phrases like "limited seats" or "join today" are pressure tactics used by scammers.

📱

WhatsApp Only

If all communication happens only on WhatsApp with no official process — be very careful.

🔍

Unverifiable Company

Always check the company on MCA India and LinkedIn. If it does not exist there — it is fake.
